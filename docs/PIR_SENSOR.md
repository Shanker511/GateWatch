# PIR Motion Sensor

The PIR input is supplemental evidence. It does not identify a person or distinguish a human from livestock, wildlife, moving vegetation, or rapid temperature changes.

## Firmware controls

- 30-second start-up warm-up
- 200 ms input debounce
- 60-second event cooldown
- Separate motion state from fence state
- Combined high-priority alert when motion occurs while the fence state is broken

## Placement

Mount the PIR below or beside the radio enclosure and point it along the approach corridor rather than directly at the sky, sun, reflective water, or rapidly heating metal. A hood reduces rain and direct solar exposure.

For first tests, use a small 3.3 V PIR module. Confirm its output voltage with a meter before connecting it to the XIAO.
