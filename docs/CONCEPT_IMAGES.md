# Concept Image Notes

The ten images in `assets/concepts/` illustrate the intended product, field use, and presentation story. They were created before the physical prototype was assembled.

They are useful for:

- explaining the fence-tension mechanism
- showing the intended enclosure and deployment
- describing multi-zone mesh coverage
- planning the demonstration video
- communicating the motion/fence event logic

They are not authoritative for:

- exact pin assignments
- electrical voltage levels
- component dimensions
- final enclosure construction
- proof that the hardware has been built

Use `docs/WIRING.md` and `hardware/wiring-reference.svg` for wiring. Replace or supplement concept images with real photographs before competition judging.
