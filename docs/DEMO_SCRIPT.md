# 60–90 Second Demo Script

1. Show the real XIAO ESP32-S3, Wio-SX1262, antenna, tension switch, and PIR sensor.
2. Show the receiver node and Meshtastic app.
3. State: “GateWatch monitors fence tension and nearby motion without cellular service.”
4. Show the fence wire under tension and confirm `SECURE`.
5. Release or cut the test wire.
6. Show the mechanical lever releasing the normally-closed microswitch.
7. Show the `FENCE BREAK` alert arriving on the receiver.
8. Trigger the PIR while the fence remains broken.
9. Show `MOTION NEAR BROKEN FENCE`.
10. Restore tension and show `FENCE RESTORED`.
11. End with a wide shot of both nodes and the actual test distance.

Do not use concept images as a substitute for the real demonstration.
