# Safety

- Do not connect the XIAO, Wio-SX1262, microswitch, PIR, or breadboard directly to an energized electric-fence conductor.
- GateWatch v0.2 uses a mechanically actuated, electrically isolated switch.
- Barbed wire can cut skin and can release stored energy when tension changes. Wear eye protection, gloves, long sleeves, and use proper fence tools.
- Install a mechanical stop so the microswitch and enclosure do not carry fence tension.
- Never transmit without the correct antenna attached.
- Do not place a breadboard prototype outdoors unattended.
- Use a protected battery, fused wiring, strain relief, weather-rated cable glands, and a sealed enclosure for field trials.
- Follow local radio-region settings and applicable fencing, electrical, workplace, and land-use requirements.
